# Selected access revision — conditional geometric candidate, NOFAB

This independent derivative preserves the four `SEATED_*` files as explicit rejected-service comparisons. Their installed solids did not collide, but the cassette driver was blocked by the new front anchor and its screw. The OD19 upper cover was also trapped by the rail-pocket inner lip. Neither observation is hidden by nominal assembly checks.

## Exact changes

- Front retainer Y screw/seat axis: **X−3.8, Z30.5** (was X−5.5). Rear remains X−3.8,Z136.2. Shaft reference 8 mm long, radius .8; head radius1.5,height1.6; actual vendor/tool/material qualification is unchanged and unresolved.
- Front lower root seat X[-5.05,-2.55], Z[29.25,31.75], Y3.35, with .85 radius model bore. Rear seat X[-5.05,-2.55], Z[134.95,137.45], sameY. Frame bottom Y3.35 is supported, not suspended on screw shanks. Effective ideal face contact per complete seat is 2.5²−π(.9²)=3.70531 mm², excluding the frame clearance hole; actual pressure/flatness not qualified.
- Front lower bridge has an explicit cassette driver passage: center X−6.3,Y−2.7, radius1.1, Z28..33. The tested tool budget is a straight radius1 shaft along Z20..61.4. This .1 mm nominal radial gap is not a printing tolerance approval. Upper bridge material above Y−1.6 remains the intended load route, not the thin lower remnant. Near the two orthogonal bores the nominal minimum separation is .55 mm (axis separation2.5 minus1.1 and.85); actual pullout/torque/creep qualification remains required.
- OD19 rail pockets now open through the split boundary: X[7.2,7.9] and[-7.9,-7.2], Y2.9..4.5, Z29..137.7. This removes the inner undercut so the nonlocating upper cover can lift without releasing the independent frame. OD20 did not require this cut.
- The already-open front collar is relieved for the relocated anchor. This is still **not a complete nose, camera mount or closed cap**. No new component has been hidden in the nine-piece count.

The three B supports, three Y-toe contact projections, side/tail contact projections, original cassette horizontal axes, long-wire paths and proposed B70.5 ports are unchanged. No new copper-contact audit is inherited for a changed footprint: none of those contact footprints changed. Mainboard F entities in the .83 sample still move−Y .10 with a separately .83-thick body; B entities stay fixed. Source only establishes nominal .83/max.93, not a guaranteed minimum.

## Service interpretation

For both variants, the upper cover is nonlocating. Remove it before introducing the two top-access driver budgets. The front collar is already open aboveY3 but must be removed for the tested axial cassette-driver route. Any future actual nose, latch, battery fixing, wire bundle or tool handle requires a fresh interference and assembly check. The tested bare shaft has no selected driver SKU or torque capability. The handle must remain outside the body; no invisible internal handle clearance is assumed.

Frame lowering is checked only in deliberately pre-deflected installed cubic poses. The real loading fixture, finger release, local edge force, printing accuracy, material properties, global-frame twist, fatigue and creep remain gates. The two unsupported bare wire tails still need actual insulation/strain relief; no tube or coating is silently selected. Electrical native implementation of the B70.5 host remains separate.

The parent ENGINEERING_CONTRACT_DRAFT documents prior design logic and rejected geometry. **For current front-axis/seat coordinates this revision takes precedence.** The nine-piece construction is a comparison, not an instruction to fabricate before all process/force/cost gates are closed. 19/20 mm are study sizes, not approved replacement for 15–18 mm.

The two final frame holes are nominal Ø1.8 around Ø1.6 reference shafts, at Z30.5/136.2 (pitch105.7 mm). Nominal radial clearance .10 mm at each means, in an otherwise ideal planar assembly, no more than .20 mm relative pitch mismatch can be absorbed by opposite use of the two clearances. This is a geometric limit, not a demonstrated print tolerance; no lower bound on actual hole diameter, shrinkage or flatness is yet qualified. Measure the finished frame/anchor pattern and reject or process-correct the single frame rather than force it into bowed alignment. A different slotted retention interface would be a new design, not silently assumed here.

## Blocking mainboard-B solder access gate

Only the carrier-side Z78.4 service windows exist. No real soldering/holding/inspection tool path to the proposed B70.5 mainboard lands at Z56.5 has been checked or provided. The seated PCB blocks approach from +Y, and the lower shell remains underneath. Thus the two-fixed-bodies-then-solder sequence is **not executable as claimed without a further design/process solution**; driver access and non-colliding wires do not close it. Do not interpret this candidate as a fully installable finished pen. Pre-soldering in an external common fixture followed by transfer is also unproven and may strain or displace the endpoints. No new tip/port is invented in this bounded study.
